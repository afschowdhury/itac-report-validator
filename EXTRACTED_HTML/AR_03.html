Recommendation

For the maximum cost and environmental benefits, our team recommends upgrading all the facility’s lighting equipment with LED lights. The advantages of LED lights are that the LED lights can work without a ballast, have much lower-rated power, and maintain their initial lumen outputs while lasting longer than fluorescent lights. Additionally, the LED lights produce much less heat when in operation. The facility can gradually phase out the current lighting equipment every time a piece burns out and mount the new LED ones. The facility can switch out the current fluorescent lighting fixtures with the replacement of LED that is rated, as shown in Table 4-9.

Calculations

Current Energy Usage 

The current fluorescent lights run for the ordinary shift hours. Therefore, current energy consumption for lighting can be estimated as follows:  

EUc = Wc × OH × CF 

= 367,400 × 6,552 hrs/yr × 0.001 kW/W

= 2,407,205 kWh/yr,

where 

            EUc = Current Annual Energy Usage, kWh/yr 

Wc = Collective Wattage of All Lights

OH = Operation Hours for Lights = 21 hrs/day × 6 days/week × 52 weeks/yr = 6,552 hrs/yr

CF = Conversion Factor (kW/W) = 0.001 

Proposed Energy Usage 

After the facility upgrades all fixtures to LED, the annual electricity usage can be calculated as follows: 

EUP = WP × OH× CF 

= 76,080 W × 6,552 hrs/yr × 0.001 kW/W

= 498,476 kWh/yr,

where  

EUP = Proposed Annual Energy Usage, kWh/yr 

WP  = Collective Wattage of Proposed LED Tubes

OH = Operation Hours for Lights = 21hrs/day × 6 days/week ×52 weeks/yr = 6,552 hrs/yr

CF = Conversion Factor (kW/W) = 0.001 

Electrical Energy Savings  

The total amount of electrical energy savings for this implementation can be calculated:  

ER  = EUC – EUP 

= 2,407,205 kWh/yr – 498,476 kWh/yr 

= 1,908,279 kWh/yr,

where 

ER = Electrical Energy Reduction, kWh/yr 

EUC  = Current Electrical Energy Usage, kWh/yr 

EUP = Proposed Electrical Energy Usage, kWh/yr 

 

Electrical Energy Cost Savings 

The total electrical energy cost savings with the replacement of all the current lights with LED lights in the facility can be estimated by the following equation:  

ECS  = ER × UR  

= 1,908,279 kWh/yr × $0.082/kWh 

= $156,516/yr,

where 

ECS  = Total Electrical Energy Cost Savings, $/yr 

ER  = Electrical Energy Reduced, kWh/yr 

UR = Average Electrical Energy Usage Rate = $0.082/kWh 

 

Electrical Demand Savings

The total amount of electrical demand savings can be calculated using the following equation:

EDS = (Wc –Wp) × CF × 12 months

= (367,400 W – 76,080 W) × 0.001 × 12 months

= 3,496 kW/yr,

where

EDS = Estimated Demand Savings, kW/yr

Wc  = Collective Wattage of Current Lights

Wp  = Collective Wattage LED Tubes

CF = Conversion Factor (kW/W) = 0.001 

Electrical Demand Cost Savings

By lowering the amount of energy, the facility uses during its peak demand period, there will be a lower demand charge. The amount of energy demand cost savings (EDCS) can be estimated using the following equation:

EDCS = EDS × DR

= 3,496 kW/yr × $4.500/kW

= $15,732 /yr,

where

EDCS = Estimated Demand Cost Savings, $/yr

EDS = Electrical Demand Savings, kW/yr

DR = Demand Charge = $4.500/kW

Total Cost Savings  

Since there has been a demand charge on the facility, the total cost savings is the sum of electrical energy cost savings and estimated demand cost savings:

TCS  = ECS + EDCS

= $156,516/yr + 15,732 /yr

= $172,248 /yr,

where 

TCS = Total Cost Savings, $/yr 

ECS = Total Electrical Energy Cost Savings, $/yr

EDCS = Estimated Demand Cost Savings, $/yr    

Carbon Dioxide Reduction  

Due to the reduced electrical usage, there will also be lower carbon dioxide emissions from the electrical power generation. These reductions can be calculated using:  

CO2  = ER × K1 × K2 

= 1,908,279 kWh/yr × (0.763 lb CO2 / kWh) × 1 Ton/2,000lbs 

= 728 Tons/yr,

where  

CO2 = Amount of Carbon Dioxide Reduced, Tons/yr 

ER  = Electrical Energy Reduction, kWh/yr 

K1 = Amount of CO2 Produced per kWh, 0.763 lb CO2/kWh 

K2 = Conversion Factor = 1 Ton/2,000 lbs  

 

Implementation Cost

Implementation will involve the labor cost required to replace the lighting fixtures, along with the material cost of the LED lighting equipment (see Figure 4-6). It is assumed that the labor cost is $25/hour, with the time needed for the replacement to be 0.5 hours per fixture for LED lamps.

Figure 4-6. Recommended LED Lighting Types

Also, Entergy has a rebate program available in LED lighting fixture retrofit. According to the prescriptive incentive rates requirement, the facility qualifies as a large commercial entity since its monthly peak demand is more than 100 kW. The incentive rates vary by bulb type, with $240/fixture for LED Corn Bulbs, $70/fixture for High Bay Lights, $80/fixture for Flood Lights, and $80/fixture for Wall Pack Lights. The total implementation cost can be estimated by:

IC = Ncorn × (UCcorn – Icorn) + Nhighbay × (UChighbay – Ihighbay) + Nflood × (UCflood – Iflood) + Nwall × (UCwall – Iwall) + LR × LT × N

= 250 × ($270/tube – $240/tube) +120 × ($145/tube – $70/tube) + 61 × ($222/tube – $80/tube) + 5 × ($101/tube – $80/tube) + $25/hour × 0.5 hours/fixture× 436 fixtures

Since the incentive can only be a maximum of $25,000

= $73,947 (material)+ $5,450 (labor) 

= $79,397

where 

IC = Implementation Cost, $ 

Ncorn = Number of LED Corn Bulbs

Nhighbay = Number of LED High Bay Lights

Nflood = Number of LED Flood Lights

Nwall = Number of LED Wall Pack Lights

UCcorn = Cost per LED Corn Bulbs, $/bulb

UChighbay = Cost per LED High Bay Lights, $/bulb

UCflood = Cost per LED Flood Lights, $/bulb

UCwall = Cost per LED Wall Pack Lights, $/bulb

UC4ftT8 = Cost per 4ft T8 LED Tube, $/tube

Icorn = Incentive Rate for Replacing LED Corn Bulbs, $/bulb

Ihighbay = Incentive Rate for Replacing LED High Bay Lights

Iflood = Incentive Rate for Replacing LED Flood Lights

Iwall = Incentive Rate for Replacing LED Wall Pack Lights

LR = Labor Rate, $/hour 

LT = Labor Time per Fixture Replacement, hour/fixture

N = Total Number of Fixtures
