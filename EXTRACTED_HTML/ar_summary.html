AR No. 1 – Install Programmable Thermostat in the Office

Installing a programmable thermostat in the office will optimize heating and cooling schedules, reducing unnecessary HVAC operation during unoccupied periods. This recommendation will yield $3,511 in annual savings and incur an implementation cost of $40, resulting in an extremely quick payback period of 0.01 years while reducing CO₂ emissions by 16 tons annually.

AR No. 2 – Utilizing Daylight Whenever Possible in Lieu of Artificial Light

Installing daylight and photocell sensors will automatically control lighting based on available daylight, eliminating unnecessary lighting operations in vacant areas. This recommendation will yield $58,573 in annual savings and incur an implementation cost of $1,218, resulting in a rapid payback period of 0.02 years while reducing CO₂ emissions by 273 tons annually.

AR No. 3 – Utilize Higher Efficiency Lamps and/or Ballasts

Upgrading to higher-efficiency lamps and ballasts will significantly improve lighting system performance while reducing energy consumption. This recommendation will yield $172,248 in annual savings and incur an implementation cost of $79,397, resulting in a favorable payback period of 0.46 years while reducing CO₂ emissions by 728 tons annually.

AR No. 4 – Upgrade Controls on Air Compressor System

Upgrading the controls on the air compressor system will optimize its operation and reduce unnecessary runtime, improving overall system efficiency. This recommendation will yield $17,101 in annual savings and incur an implementation cost of $12,500, resulting in a favorable payback period of 0.73 years while reducing CO₂ emissions by 80 tons annually.
